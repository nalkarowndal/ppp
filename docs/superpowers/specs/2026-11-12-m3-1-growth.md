# M3-1 Growth Design

Date: 2026-11-12. Engine: Godot 4.7.2 GDScript. Base: feature/m2-5-battle-ui.

Pure policy layer only. Exposes new_hero, level_cap, try_level_up, try_ascend, stats_for. Successful operations return new hero and wallet dictionaries. Rejected operations return immutable snapshots and a reason, never a partial debit.

Level-up uses the existing progression.gd costs. Promotion requires reaching the star-dependent cap, does not reset level and costs gold plus stones. Rarity is independent of star. HP, ATK and DEF scale with level and one noncumulative star multiplier; SPD/CRIT/CRIT_DMG do not.

Not implemented: Equipment, idle rewards, credit ledger, save migration, and growth UI. Do not merge into main before prerequisite QA.
